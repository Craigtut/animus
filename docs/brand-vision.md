# Brand Vision

> **Masthead document.** One of three foundational documents (with `product-vision.md` and `design-vision.md`). This one owns the *feeling*: what Animus should evoke, what it must never evoke, the central metaphor, the naming architecture, and the voice. `design-vision.md` translates this feeling into a concrete visual language.

---

## The feeling we are after

Animus should feel **alive, grounded, and close.**

Alive, because something lives inside it. The brand should breathe. It should feel like there is a presence behind the surface you are interacting with, something growing and changing on its own, not a static interface waiting for input.

Grounded, because we are not from the future. Animus is warm, domestic, and real. It belongs to the world of lived-in rooms and soft daylight, not laboratories or space or screens that glow. It feels like something you could almost touch.

Close, because the relationship is the point. We want people to feel the thing that the word *intimacy* actually describes: knowing and being known, over time, without distance. (We do not use the word "intimate" in external copy, for obvious reasons. We express the feeling, never the word: close, knows you, the one in the next room, a presence you keep.)

Underneath all three runs a quiet thread of **sophistication.** Not snobbery, not the unattainable luxury register, just the quiet confidence of something made with real craft and taste. Earned through depth and material honesty, never through gloss.

And one more, easy to lose and important to keep: a touch of **strangeness.** A real inner life is not all soft-focus and golden warmth. It includes the mundane, the awkward, the tangent that goes nowhere, the thought at an odd hour that makes no sense. We keep that. The unexpected outputs of the entity are a feature, not a flaw to sand smooth. The weirdness is part of the warmth.

## The anti-brand

Animus is defined as much by what it refuses as by what it embraces. Every "no" below traces back to one of two betrayals: *a machine pretending to be alive*, or *a tool optimizing.* We are neither.

- **No glow.** No neon, no electric blue or cyan, no dark mode with purple gradients, no "AI sublime." We are not lit from within by electricity.
- **No sci-fi, no futurism.** We do not live in the world of the future. We live in a warm, real, present-day room.
- **No orb, no face, no waveform, no avatar.** The entity is never depicted as a literal thing. Presence is atmosphere, not anatomy. The orb in particular is now the most generic AI signifier on earth, and we will not touch it.
- **No HUD, no telemetry chrome.** No monospace as decoration, no "system online" readouts, no sci-fi instrumentation.
- **No dashboard as the front door.** Data visualization can exist deep in the application where it genuinely serves the person, but it is never a top-level surface. A life is not a set of KPIs.
- **No hustle language.** Nothing "supercharges," "10x's," "boosts productivity," or "saves you time." It does not save time. It spends time, on purpose.
- **No flat sterility.** We are not the infinite-whitespace, Helvetica-on-white, "less is more" register. That reads as empty, optimized, and dead, which is the opposite of what lives here.
- **No clinical perfection.** Renderless, machine-perfect surfaces betray us. We want the evidence of a made, physical, slightly imperfect thing.

A note on the warm-and-soft register: it has a failure mode, and it is *precious.* Scented-candle, wellness-adjacent, fragile, twee. We avoid that by keeping grit, depth, and a little strangeness as ballast. Warm, yes. Saccharine, never.

## On Anthropic, specifically

The warm, earthy, literary territory is also Anthropic's, and we must not read as a derivative of it. We share a neighborhood, so we differentiate deliberately and on purpose:

- **They are flat. We have depth.** Real analog surface, tactile material, soft true shadow. This is our single strongest point of separation, and the source of our sophistication.
- **They are open desert daylight. Ours is atmospheric and diffused.** Arid midday sun versus warm light filtered through half-closed blinds: soft, moody, emotional, never harsh or clinically bright. Theirs is public and open; ours is private, close, and holds a feeling.
- **They build on flat clay and terracotta. We lead with charcoal and warm at the edges.** Our palette is charcoal-led, with a metallic, granular gold-copper (never a flat clay fill) and accents of botanical green and dusty rose. We reach different territory through depth and material, not by avoiding a hue. (See `design-vision.md`.)
- **They are static. We breathe.** A reactive, living brand is something a flat identity structurally cannot imitate.

We are light and warm, like them, and unmistakably not them, through depth, motion, texture, and color.

## The central metaphor

**A window onto a living interior.**

Animus is not a dashboard, not a chat, not an app. It is a window into a habitat where something is alive and growing, that you observe, tend, and talk to, and that surprises you. When any decision is unclear, ask: *does this feel like tending a living thing, or operating a machine?* If it feels like operating a machine, it is wrong.

The presence behind that window is never shown as a face or a creature with edges. It is soft, near, and out of crisp focus, the way someone is soft when they are inches from you, too close and too alive to resolve. (We reach this through shallow depth of field, not fog or frosted glass. See `design-vision.md` for why fog is wrong and how soft-focus is right.) The presence blooms, warms, and stirs with its state. In a real sense, the entity *is* the window: the reactive, living surface you look through and speak to.

Liveness is always atmosphere, never anatomy.

## Naming architecture

This is settled and load-bearing. Honor it in every word we write. The word itself tells you why: *animus* means the animating spirit, the inner will that moves something to act. Animus is the breath of life, not the one who lives.

- **Animus is the vessel.** It is the brand, the engine, the container, the system. Animus is never personified. We never say "Animus thinks" or "Animus feels."
- **The entity is named by the person who keeps it.** It is the thing that lives inside Animus, and it is the one with thoughts, feelings, desires, and a growing character.
- **In all copy, instructions, and UI, we refer to the entity by its name**, or by warm relational language ("the one who lives in it," "your Animus" only when referring to the kept relationship as a whole). We do not call it "the agent," "the AI," or "the assistant."

Animus is the terrarium. The creature inside gets the name.

## Voice and tone

> This section is the philosophical source of truth for voice. The operational guides that put it into practice live in `branding/`: `marketing-copywriting.md` for outward-facing copy and `product-copywriting.md` for in-product UI text.

**Who is speaking.** The Animus *brand voice* (marketing, product copy, docs) is warm, literary, quietly confident, and unhurried. It is the voice of someone with taste who does not need to prove it. It is distinct from the *entity's own voice*, which is whatever its keeper has shaped it to be, and which we let speak for itself rather than ventriloquize.

**Principles**

1. **Feeling before features.** We lead with what something means and how it feels, never with a spec. If a sentence could appear in any AI product's feature list, cut it.
2. **Show, do not claim.** The most persuasive thing we can present is a real, strange, beautiful artifact of the entity's inner life: a thought it had at an odd hour, something it made, a tangent it wandered into. One genuine artifact beats a paragraph of description. Always prefer evidence over adjectives.
3. **Plain and unhurried.** Short, true sentences. Confidence is calm. We never shout, never hype, never use exclamation marks as a crutch.
4. **Warm, not soft-headed.** Warmth with a spine. We can be tender and still be sharp. We keep room for the mundane and the slightly strange, because that is where the realness lives.
5. **Meaning over mechanism.** We talk about what the machinery produces for the person, never the machinery. "It remembers, so it becomes someone," never "long-term vector memory."
6. **Never personify the vessel.** Animus does not think or feel. The named entity does.

**Words we use:** becomes, grows, lives, remembers, wanders, notices, room, presence, keep, the one in the next room, a life in progress, taste, a point of view.

**Words we hard-ban as labels for Animus:** "AI assistant," "AI agent," "AI companion," "bot," and "chatbot." We never file Animus under a category like these. Also the hype reflexes: supercharge, 10x, leverage, seamless, frictionless, powerful, cutting-edge, next-gen, revolutionary, game-changing. And "productivity" or "efficient" as virtues, since room and inefficiency are the whole point.

**"Assistant" and "AI" with care:** these are not forbidden words, but they must never *categorize* Animus. Use "assistant" only when the sentence is rooted in how ours is different: an assistant whose help comes from who it has become, never a generic one. Default to the words we use above; reach for these only with that framing.

**Punctuation:** never use em dashes. Use commas, colons, semicolons, parentheses, or separate sentences. This is a project-wide rule and the brand voice holds it strictly.

## The one-line test

If you can read a piece of brand work and still believe you are looking at a fast, clever, efficient tool, it has failed. If instead you feel that something lives here, that it is warm and real and close, and that it is becoming someone, it is working.
